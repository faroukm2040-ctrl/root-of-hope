WHO WE ARE

Hope grows when people are supported.

Root of Hope Organization works toward a more just and inclusive society where people facing vulnerability can access support, opportunity and respect.

Our mission is to empower the girl child, older persons, persons with disabilities and people experiencing homelessness by promoting human rights and justice for all.

OUR APPROACH

Listen. Include. Empower.

We believe sustainable change begins by listening to people, respecting their rights and working alongside communities. We aim to build partnerships that turn shared commitment into practical support.

  • Put dignity and human rights first
  • Include people whose voices are often overlooked
  • Work collaboratively with communities and partners
  • Use resources responsibly and transparently
